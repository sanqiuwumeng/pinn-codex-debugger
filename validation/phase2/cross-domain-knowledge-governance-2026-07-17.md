## Evidence comparison

The same evidence-driven sequence was exercised in the declared thermal and
viscous Burgers cases:

1. validate the physical model, units, reference and metric contract;
2. analyze the aligned baseline field and localize its maximum error;
3. parameterize exactly one fixed-collocation intervention from that diagnosis;
4. run smoke before full execution;
5. let the case-specific contract accept or reject each frozen seed;
6. preserve negative evidence, provenance and zero-relaunch replay.

The scientific outcomes contradict any universal focused-collocation claim:

| Case | Accepted seeds | Rejected seeds | Acceptance rate |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Thermal | 7, 42 | 2026 | 2/3 |
| Viscous Burgers | 2026 | 7, 42 | 1/3 |

The repeated pattern is therefore the governed diagnostic workflow, not a
claim that localized sampling always improves a PINN.
